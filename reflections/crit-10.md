# Crit 10 — Fly by instruments

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

I noticed that when I run several sessions in the background, I lose track of what Claude is doing, even for things I discussed with it in detail. I would agree an approach, come back later and not remember it. A small example: I could not remember which parts of the hand and arm are tracked for a tennis swing, so I had to ask again and re-prompt. The breakthrough was seeing why. The decisions lived in a chat I could no longer see, not somewhere I could go back to. So the docs (CLAUDE.md, the task files, the devlog, the ADR) are not only for Claude or for a marker. They are what I fall back on, and they let me pick up cleanly without redoing the conversation.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

I used to think the way to stay in control was to remember everything. With several sessions running at once that doesn't work, and I don't think it has to. The tool is good at reminding me: I can ask what is tracked and it answers straight away, which is a strange new kind of lookup. So I want to keep the record clear enough for me to fall back on, and also use the tool as a memory. I do need to stay in charge of the decisions and why they were made. That has to be written down where I can find it, because I can't hold all of it in my head.
